Speech bubble

Interview (onboarding, plan reveal): surface fill, 1 px line border, radius 24, padding 16 × 18. Text lg 20/26 at 600; drop to md 16/22 at 500 past about 80 characters. Rana sits left at 96–124 px wide, bottom-aligned, 10 px gap.

Tip (every other screen): 44 px avatar (36 px during a workout), radius 16, padding 10 × 14, text sm 14/20. Under 90 characters and always specific: a weight, a rep count or an exercise.

Tail: a 12–14 px square rotated 45°, left and bottom borders only, 7–8 px outside the left edge. Bottom 22 for interview, top 13–15 for tips so it points at the avatar's mouth.

No shadow. One bubble per screen. Interview bubbles fade and rise 8 px over 240 ms when the question changes; nothing else on the screen animates.

Progress bar

Level: 10 px tall, line track, accent fill, full radius. Always shows the numbers under it: XP so far over the next level's total on the left, XP remaining on the right. Level n needs 44 × (n − 1)² XP, per docs/leveling-system.md.

Lift: 6 px. The bar spans start to target, so fill = (current − start) ÷ (target − start). Start, gain and target sit under it.

Rest timer: 6 px with an ink fill, so it never reads as progress toward a goal. Steps (exercise 2 of 6, onboarding): 4 px segments, 4 px gaps.

Use role="progressbar" with min, max and now. Fills animate width over 240 ms only when the value changes.

Buttons

Primary: 56 tall, full radius, accent fill, ink text md 16/700. One per screen, pinned to the bottom with 24 px side margins. Pressed #E0940F; disabled uses line fill and muted text.

Secondary: 56 tall (44 compact), surface fill, 1 px line border, ink 16/600. For the quieter path, such as starting on a rest day.

Text: 44 px hit area. Ink with a 3 px underline offset for opt-outs; accent-strong 700 for header actions like Finish. Icon: 44 × 44, 24 px glyph, aria-label required.

Labels say what happens: "Start workout", "Next: pull-up", "Start my plan". No arrows appended, no all caps.

How to do it

Cards

Hero card: surface, radius 24, padding 20, no border, no shadow. At most one per screen (Today's workout, Strength).

List card: radius 20, padding 4 × 18, rows 12 px top and bottom with a divider #ECEDE7 between them.

Option card (onboarding): radius 20, 1 px line border, 72 min height. Selected: 2 px accent border, wash fill, filled check. Single-choice questions advance on tap after a short pause.

Collapsible: radius 16, a 60 px button header with aria-expanded; the chevron turns 180° over 160 ms and the body pushes content down.

Everything else sits straight on chalk. If two cards side by side look the same, one of them should be a list.

Pull-up Lat pulldown

Inputs

Number field: 60 tall in onboarding (label above, sm 14/600; unit suffix muted), 48 tall in set rows (radius 12, centered). Value lg 20/700, tabular figures. Digits only with a numeric keypad, no spinners: keep using Rana.ui.numericField.

States: focus = 2 px ink border plus a 3 px accent-soft ring. Error = 2 px #B3261E border and a message under it that says how to fix it. Logged sets drop their border and fill so finished numbers read as plain text.

Chips (exercise options): 40 tall, full radius. Selected uses the same accent border and wash as option cards. A solid ink chip only marks the current step.
